# 👋 Hello there ! I'm Camille Robichon

🎓 PhD Candidate in Food Science (New ways of formulating vitamin E to enhance its antioxidant efficiency in food matrix)  
🧪 Researcher working on **oxidative stability in emulsions**, **antioxidant interactions**, **antioxidant formulation** and **kinetic modelling**.  
💻 I build **MATLAB tools** to analyse complex degradation curves and compare kinetic models in a transparent, reproducible way.

## 🧮 Modelling & code

I like turning experimental curves into interpretable parameters.

**Main repositories:**
- 📄 **CV** 
  > [Download CV (PDF)](https://github.com/camillerobichon30/camillerobichon30-cv/blob/main/cv.pdf)

- 📊 **Antioxidant Kinetic Model Comparison**  
  `Antioxidant-Kinetics-Model-Comparison`  
  > Comparative fitting of **Weibull, Gompertz, Logistic and Exponential** models.  
  > Includes AIC/BIC, Akaike weights and Vuong tests to identify the statistically best model per condition.
